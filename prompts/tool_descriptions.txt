search_documents: Use for Apple historical figures, risk factors, financial statements,
and Apple management discussion in the local 10-K.

search_web: Use only for current or external information not available in the Apple 10-K,
such as Microsoft's latest gross margin. Prefer primary sources.

calculate_financial_metric: Use after retrieval. Supply only registry fact IDs or
calculation IDs from prior tools. Use percentage for ratios, gross_margin_percentage for
(revenue - cost of revenue) / revenue, and percentage_point_difference for comparisons.
